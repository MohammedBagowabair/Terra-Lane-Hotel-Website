The idea

A small hotel with a big neighbourhood.

Terra Lane is a hotel concept set in a row of pre-war shophouses. We kept the tall shutters, timber stairs and five-foot way, then added quiet rooms, good beds and a team who know where the best laksa is. No pool, no spa: just a comfortable base for exploring the city on foot.

  • Restored heritage architecture
  • Hawker food within a few minutes’ walk
  • Friendly local tips from our team

FAQ

Good to know

How do I check availability?

Message us on WhatsApp with your dates and number of guests. We reply with the rooms available and current rates before anything is confirmed.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 3:00 pm and check-out is until 11:00 am. Ask us about early arrival or a late check-out and we will help when rooms allow.

Is there a pool or spa?

No. Terra Lane is a small shophouse hotel focused on comfortable rooms and the neighbourhood around it. We are happy to suggest nearby options.

Is breakfast available?

Many guests prefer the local breakfast stalls nearby. We share our favourite picks at check-in, and simple grab-and-go breakfast can be arranged on request.

Can I store luggage before check-in?

Yes. Luggage storage is free on your arrival and departure days.

How do I get there from the airport?

By MRT, taxi or a private transfer we can arrange on request. Send us your flight details and we will share the simplest route.
